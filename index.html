About me

The person
behind the dream

Hey, I'm Sani — a student from Bangladesh, currently in Class 11 in the Science department, with my HSC journey coming up ahead. Right now my days are books, classes and the occasional (very necessary) break.

When I'm not studying, you'll find me lost in anime and movies — two of my favourite ways to wander through worlds I haven't seen yet. I'm endlessly fascinated by mountains, far-away cities, different cultures and the kind of places that look unreal in photographs.

My biggest dream? To travel around the world. I haven't set off yet — this site is the map I'm drawing before the first step.
